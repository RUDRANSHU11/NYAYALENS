EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 April 2026 between Horizon Technologies Private Limited ("the Company") and Ananya Rao ("the Employee").

1. APPOINTMENT
The Company appoints the Employee as a Software Engineer at its Pune office. The Employee shall report to the Engineering Manager and shall perform the duties reasonably assigned to that role.

2. COMPENSATION
The Company shall pay the Employee a gross salary of Rs. 10,80,000 per annum, payable monthly in arrears on or before the 7th day of each month. The salary shall be reviewed once in every 18 months.

3. PROBATION
The Employee shall be on probation for 6 months from the date of joining. The Company may extend the probation period by a further 3 months by written notice.

4. NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 60 days written notice to the other party.

5. TERMINATION
The Company may terminate this Agreement without notice if the Employee commits misconduct, breaches confidentiality, or remains absent without approval for more than 3 consecutive days.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee shall not disclose any confidential information or proprietary information of the Company to any third party during employment and for 3 years after this Agreement ends.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product created by the Employee in the course of employment shall be the exclusive intellectual property of the Company, including any copyright in it.

8. LEAVE
The Employee is entitled to 18 days of paid leave in each calendar year. Unused leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration at Pune in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

11. NON-COMPETE
For 12 months after this Agreement ends, the Employee shall not be employed by, or provide services to, any business that competes with the Company within India.

12. PENALTY FOR EARLY EXIT
If the Employee resigns within 12 months of joining, the Employee shall pay the Company liquidated damages of Rs. 1,00,000 towards training costs.
